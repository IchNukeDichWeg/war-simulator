**v0.1.0 -- first public release.** Pit states from any era against each other on a real-world map and
watch the war play out day by day in the browser. This release follows a pre-release sweep: parallel reviews
of the run lifecycle, Setup and import, the engine, the data and the whole git history for leaks, with
every engine fix behind its own toggle and measured against the backtest.

```
Tests    | 288 passing in 39 files, typecheck clean
Backtest | 24 historical battles x 20 seeds: winner 19/24, loss ratio 15/24, duration 22/24
Presets  | 23 of 24 screened at 10 seeds (a kill filter, not a measurement)
Data     | 1,234 snapshots (345 researched, 889 derived), 2250 BC to 2026
Bundle   | dist 31.5 MB, of which 29.1 MB is runtime data; JS 493 kB gzip
```

### What is in it
- 24 ready-made matchups and a Setup panel for any states, years, theater and victory rules.
- Coalitions, own-era or equalized technology, Monte Carlo odds, and an explanation of every result.
- A deterministic day-step engine in a Web Worker on a 0.25 degree grid with terrain, weather, daylight,
  supply, sieges, sea lift and fog of war.
- Save and share by file or link, and an Editor for any snapshot value.
- A Settings menu: basemap shading, unit size, the speed a run starts at, and remembered map layers.

### Pre-release fixes
- **Run lifecycle (13):** stale worker messages are dropped by run id, a forward seek keeps the skipped
  days' events, an ended run reopens when seeking back, and "run longer" reruns the war that ran.
- **Setup and import (13):** duplicate snapshots refused, theater boxes kept between the poles, side
  colours limited to `#rrggbb`, the seed kept within 32 bits, and startup survives malformed data.
- **Security:** the built page carries a Content Security Policy with no inline scripts or styles.
- **Engine, combat and movement (7):** air interdiction flies from the side that holds the air, sea lift
  is checked where formations embark, land links that only touch at a corner of water are cut, and
  embarked formations stay out of land battles they are not landing in.
- **Engine, campaign core (8):** coalition morale collapse is judged per member, the depot sea lift waits
  for the seed waves, a broken member can recover, and backtest reinforcements count elapsed days.
- **Data:** borders pinned to one upstream commit so a clean clone rebuilds the same files, five
  micro-states keep their polygons, Kosovo resolves its border and area, and the road network series
  under a non-free licence was removed.

### Results that moved
- **France 1940** (backtest): Germany wins 95% of 20 seeds, was 80%; 53 days, was 48 (historical 43).
- **NATO vs Warsaw Pact 1989** (preset, 10 seeds): NATO 8/2/0, median day 201, was 9/1/0 with a median
  of 37: the Warsaw Pact no longer collapses on morale while only East Germany is on the map.
- **Argentina vs United Kingdom 1982** (preset, 10 seeds): 2/7/1 (Argentina/UK/draw). The sea-lift fix
  `SEA_LIFT_AFTER_SEED_WAVES` is measured and tested but ships OFF: with it ON the British reach the islands
  27 days later and the preset reads 4/2/4.

### Known limits
- The map imagery and elevation tiles load from NASA GIBS and AWS at runtime; offline, the map is blank
  but the simulation still runs.
- Backtest misses: winner at Carrhae, Marathon, Mohi, the Six-Day War and Yom Kippur (Sinai); loss ratio
  in 9 cases; duration at El Alamein and Tannenberg. See VALIDATION.md.
- Four battle cases (France 1940, the Falklands, Stalingrad, Yom Kippur) list historical losses above the
  personnel they field.
- Some derived border polygons are wrong for their year: Parthia -53, Habsburg Austria 1700, the Russian
  Empire 1760, Liao 1004, the Maratha Empire, Rome and Carthage -218, Axum and Ghana.
- The Maldives cannot deploy: its atolls are water cells on the terrain grid.
- With `SEA_LIFT_AFTER_SEED_WAVES` OFF, a sea-entry member's depot troops can use the same ships as its
  day-0 seed waves (the UK lifts 143,640 men on day 24, one week after its first landing, with a 33.8-day
  round trip). The Falklands preset's British win rests on that double booking.
- Armies cannot march in polar night (0 hours of daylight); they can now fight an adjacent enemy.
- A reinforcement whose spawn cells are all enemy-held is skipped and never retried (Stalingrad's day-23
  Soviet group in seed 1).
- Preset quirks: the UK sea lift warning reads "0-day round trip", and the United States moves overland in
  Japan vs USA 1941. Central Powers vs Entente was not in the preset screen.
- Re-running the Phase 3 enumeration does not reproduce the committed derived snapshots byte for byte.
- COVERAGE.md's derived count (889 of 952, 70 skipped) does not add up exactly.
- The CSP blocks inline styles; every exercised path works, but an untested MapLibre feature could need
  `'unsafe-inline'` added back in `vite.config.ts`.
