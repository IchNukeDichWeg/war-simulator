**v0.1.1 -- an army marching in shows its real morale.** A display fix on top of v0.1.0. The live
panel no longer reads 0% morale and supply for a side whose whole army is still on its way to the
theater. No result changes.

```
Tests    | 289 passing in 40 files, typecheck clean
Backtest | 24 historical battles x 20 seeds: winner 19/24, loss ratio 15/24, duration 22/24 (unchanged)
Oracle   | Rome vs Spain 2026 seed 1, Rome morale readout days 1-28: 0% -> 75%
Base     | v0.1.0
```

### What changed
- The side readout averaged morale and supply over formations on the map only, so a side with none on
  the map read 0%. Rome vs Spain 2026 showed Rome at 0% morale for its first 28 days while its 90
  formations marched in at 75%. The readout now uses the marching formations until one arrives.
- It is a display fix only: victory and the result explanations read the formations directly, so every
  outcome is the same as in v0.1.0.

### Known limits
- Everything listed for v0.1.0 still applies, see `docs/releases/v0.1.0.md`.
- The fallback covers a side with no formation on the map. While part of an army is on the map and
  part is still marching in, the readout shows the arrived part only.
