### Known limits

- Population: a modern country's OWID population where polity and country overlap mutually by 70% (266 snapshots), else habitable land (outside Koppen zone E) x regional density x state-density factor, bounded by the regional total. Without a match desert counts as habitable, so fragments or supersets of desert countries (the 1945 Libya pieces, Western Sahara, Arabia 1878, Air) keep regional densities far above plausible values, and a union matched to one member (Denmark-Norway to Norway) leaves the others out.
- No horses (cavalry, chariots, draft animals 0) in 16 polities before contact (Americas before 1493, Oceania before 1788). Post-contact horse adoption dates are not modelled per people: later polities take the peer pool's cavalry share.
- 245 snapshots have no sea access (lake shores do not count) and field no ships.
- Open: cavalry of Sub-Saharan forest-zone states (Asante and similar) comes from the regional peer pool; no fetched source on their horse use was applied.
